Game Codex

Knowledge is power. Study the core components of your fleet and the threats you will face. Master these to dominate the battlefield.

🚀

Fighter

A swift and versatile combat vessel. Cheap to deploy, with a 30% chance to fire a second shot, making it an unpredictable and high-value asset in the early game.

Cost: 1 | ATK: 2 | HP: 3

🛸

Cruiser

A balanced mid-range ship equipped with nanite repair systems. Upon deployment, it immediately restores 3 HP to your command center.

Cost: 2 | ATK: 3 | HP: 5

🚁

Battleship

A heavy-hitter armed with explosive ordnance. Its splash damage capability increases its attack power for each enemy ship on the field.

Cost: 3 | ATK: 5 | HP: 7

🛠️

Repair Drone

An essential support unit. It doesn't attack, but automatically restores 3 HP to your base at the end of every turn it survives on the battlefield.

Cost: 2 | ATK: 0 | HP: 4

⏩

Overclock

Push your systems to the limit. Instantly draw two cards, but suffer an energy penalty of -1 on your next turn. A high-risk, high-reward tactical play.

Cost: 1 | Effect: Draw 2 Cards

🛰️💥

Orbital Strike

Call in a devastating strike from orbit, dealing a massive 25 damage directly to the enemy fleet's command. This powerful card is consumed upon use.

Cost: 3 | Effect: 25 Damage

💨

EMP Pulse

Unleash a non-damaging electromagnetic pulse that temporarily disables enemy weapon systems, reducing all enemy ships' attack power by 2 for one turn.

Cost: 2 | Effect: Weaken All Enemies

💀

Pirate Scout

A weak but numerous early-wave nuisance. Their only intent is to attack, making them predictable but dangerous in large numbers.

ATK: 1 | HP: 2

☠️

Marauder

A more versatile threat than the Scout. Marauders will mix aggressive attacks with defensive maneuvers, making them tougher to take down efficiently.

ATK: 2 | HP: 3

🔥

Warship

A significant threat that can power itself up for a devastating blow. Prioritize eliminating Warships before they can execute their buffed attack.

ATK: 3 | HP: 5

👹

Mothership

The pinnacle of early-game threats. It combines high stats with unpredictable behavior, switching between attacking, defending, and buffing itself. A true test of your fleet.

ATK: 5 | HP: 8
